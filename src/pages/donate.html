Right now, families in our community are waiting for housing. You can help today.

Help break the cycle of homelessness one family at a time. While we have made great strides in our efforts, we still have a lot to accomplish.

Give online now

Online donations are processed securely through Give Lively.

We need your help to continue meeting our mission.

Our ability to continue to provide housing assistance depends on grants and donations from agencies and the local community. Please help us continue this vital work. That is why we'd like you to consider donating.

Individual or business contributions are always welcome. Consider a donation in honor of a birthday, anniversary or retirement celebration.

Other ways you can help

  • Fund-raising through your work or community service groups.
  • A donation to Bridges to Housing in place of a birthday or Christmas present. We will provide a gift card for you.
  • In-kind donation of products, time and/or services.
  • Schedule a speaker from Bridges to Housing to talk to your organization. If you are interested in having your group or business learn more about our program, please call to schedule a Bridges to Housing representative to give a short presentation at one of your meetings.

Where your gift goes

We help families secure housing by assisting with a rental deposit and/or a first month's rent and landlord incentives. The funds we provide go directly to the landlord or property owner.

Bridges to Housing is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation. Federal Tax ID 83-0490654.
