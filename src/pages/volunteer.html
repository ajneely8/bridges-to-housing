Volunteer

Volunteers are a vital part of our program's success.

We welcome volunteers from all walks of life. Three hours a week at our Yuba City office helps a local family take the first step toward a home of their own.

Email us about volunteering Call {{site.phone}}

What our volunteers do

Volunteers are asked to commit to three hours of service per week. Volunteers assist with client intake interviews, verifying information, following up with funded clients, general office work, and fundraising. All activities take place at our office, located in the Hands of Hope facility, 909 Spiva Ave., Yuba City, CA 95991.

Bridges to Housing volunteers interview our families as well as perform general office duties. Volunteers must be 18 years or older and are fingerprinted.

If you would like more information about a volunteer role, please send us an email at {{site.email}} or call {{site.phone}}.

  • Client intake interviews
  • Verifying application information
  • Following up with funded clients
  • General office work
  • Fundraising and events

Community volunteering

There are several places in our community to be an active participant.

Bridges to Housing is one of many local organizations that depend on volunteers. If our schedule is not the right fit, these Yuba-Sutter groups would welcome your time.

  • JustServe

    JustServe is all about connecting volunteers to service opportunities. JustServe.org is a website where the volunteer needs of organizations are posted, and volunteers search for opportunities to enhance the quality of life in the community. JustServe is a free service that links community volunteer needs with volunteers and does not discriminate based on race, religion, gender, ethnicity, or sexual orientation in posting projects or in encouraging volunteers to serve.

    justserve.org

  • Yuba-Sutter Domestic Animal Disaster Assistance

    Yuba Sutter Domestic Animal Disaster Assistance (YSDADA) works jointly with governmental, non-governmental, and private agencies and organizations and the public in the Yuba-Sutter area to promote the safety and well-being of domestic pets and farm animals affected by a governmentally-declared disaster.

    ysdada.org/get-involved/volunteer

  • SAYLove

    SAYLove is a grassroots citizen organization formed in 2018 by Jeff Stephens, to care and support the Yuba-Sutter area while also serving as a vehicle for change. Its main purpose is to enhance the beauty and the environment in which we live. Through community outreach, community projects and visibility, SAYLove strives to make positive changes to our area.

    info@saylove.org

  • Trauma Intervention Program

    "Providing Support after Tragedy Strikes." TIP provides immediate emotional and practical support to survivors of tragedy. Local residents are trained to be TIP Responders and are available on a 24/7/365 day basis to assist law enforcement, firefighters, paramedics, deputy coroners and hospital personnel.

    yubasuttertip.org

  • Yuba Sutter Arts

    Our volunteer positions are as varied as our volunteers. Anyone of any age can volunteer at Yuba Sutter Arts; you don't have to be an artist to support the arts in our community! We personalize our volunteer work based on your skills or what your interests are.

    yubasutterarts.org/support/volunteer

  • Yuba Sutter Food Bank

    We welcome volunteers ages 11 to 17 with an adult and anyone 18 to 80 able to lift 25 lbs. We have something for everyone to serve our community and help us end hunger in the Yuba Sutter area.

    feedingys.org/volunteering
