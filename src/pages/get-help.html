How can we help?

If a deposit is standing between your family and a home, we may be able to help.

If you and your family are struggling to get into a permanent place to call home, Bridges to Housing may be able to help you with a deposit. The first step is a conversation.

What to expect

First of all, we will listen without judgment to your story to learn about your current situation, identify your housing needs and challenges, and suggest options for you. We may be able to help you with a deposit to move into rental housing suited to your family size. We also offer referrals to other community services such as clothing, food, and child support.

Each client's story and needs are unique to that person, so it's not possible on this page to predict if and how we might be able to help a specific individual or family. So we encourage you to call or stop by the office.

How the process works

  1. Apply at the office

    Drop by the office for an application. Our Program Administrator assists clients in assembling the documents for their application and sets a date and time for an interview by a board member or volunteer.

  2. Locate housing

    Our Housing Navigator will guide you as you locate a suitable residence. Once you have found housing, the approval process will be expedited.

  3. Board review and funding

    When the interview is completed and housing has been located, the file is reviewed by the board for approval and appropriate funding. We assist with a rental deposit and/or a first month's rent and landlord incentives. The funds we provide go directly to the landlord or property owner.

Who we serve

Since 2008, we have been helping low-income, disabled, homeless, and families at risk of becoming homeless in Yuba, Sutter, and Colusa Counties to obtain the safety and security of the most basic human necessity. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, gender expression, age, national origin, disability, marital status, sexual orientation or military status.

Call or visit us

Our office is open for walk-ins. We will answer your questions and assist you in applying for our services.
