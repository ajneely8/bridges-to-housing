About us

Moving families from temporary shelter into stable housing since 2008.

Bridges to Housing, Inc. was established in 2008 as a non-profit corporation in response to the many challenges of homelessness in our community.

Our mission

Safe, decent and affordable housing for families who need it most.

Our focus is on a key element of proven solutions: moving families from transitional or otherwise temporary shelters into stable housing. Our mission is to provide assistance for low-income and homeless families in obtaining safe, decent, and affordable housing in Yuba, Sutter, and Colusa Counties, partnering with a wide variety of community resources.

Our role is to evaluate the clients' needs, and provide qualified applicants assistance with a security deposit. Working with a wide variety of community resources and social service agencies, we help families secure housing by assisting with a rental deposit and/or a first month's rent and landlord incentives. The funds we provide go directly to the landlord or property owner.

Funded entirely by grants and donations, and fueled by the hard work and dedication of our volunteers and staff, we have since 2013 successfully placed hundreds of Yuba-Sutter-Colusa families in traditional apartments or houses. That housing is then the door to other forms of support and opportunity.

A secure home is the first step in becoming a productive member of the community and providing a stable environment for children. Often, families can afford the rent but are unable to save enough money for the deposit. Other families need temporary, limited assistance with rent during a crisis.

Community partners

Housing is the door. Our partners help families walk through it.

Leadership

Our Board of Directors

Bridges to Housing is guided by community members with decades of service in Yuba-Sutter. They interview families, review applications, raise funds, and give their time freely.

President

Marsha Krouse-Taylor

Marsha Krouse-Taylor was raised in the Yuba/Sutter Community from middle school. She and her husband moved away for a time and moved back to the community in 1976. She took a job with Casa de Esperanza in 1980 and accepted the position of Executive Director in 1986, continuing in that position until August 2020. She began working with homelessness and was one of the founding members of the Yuba-Sutter Homeless Coalition, serving as Vice President for several years. Marsha joined the board of Bridges to Housing after leaving her employment at Casa de Esperanza. Serving homeless individuals as well as those at risk, victims of domestic violence, sexual assault and child abuse remains her passion.

Vice President

Anne Fletcher

Anne Fletcher has been active in community service since 1975. Serving on Casa de Esperanza's board was her first involvement in giving to the community. Anne volunteered to solicit funds for United Way, later serving as board President. She was selected to serve on the California State Bar's Judicial Nominees Evaluation Commission as a public member. Anne and a group of volunteers concerned with the lack of housing for homeless women and children established Bridges to Housing in 2008. Anne also saw a need in the Hispanic community and founded the Alliance for Hispanic Advancement. After retiring from the local office of California Rural Legal Assistance, Anne was selected and continues to serve on the California Rural Legal Assistance Board of Directors.

Board member

Sharon Foote

Sharon Foote keeps busy wearing many hats. Currently serving as a member of the Board of Directors, she also serves as a board member of the Yuba Sutter Arts Council and the Sutter County Community Action Agency, where she served as President in 2010. Additionally, she is the Chair of the Sutter County Democratic Central Committee and a member of the Alliance for Hispanic Advancement. As a board member of the annual Bok Kai Parade, Sharon oversees the activities of the reviewing stand. Sharon says she desires to provide the best possible resources to all community members by giving her time and experience.

Our commitments

Open to everyone in our community.

These include but are not limited to hiring or firing of staff, selection of volunteers and vendors, and provision of services. Bridges to Housing is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit corporation, Federal Tax ID 83-0490654. Although we welcome the donations received from religious and political organizations, we are secular, non-partisan and independent of any religious or political group.
