A local lifeline

The deposit is often the only thing standing between a family and a home.

While homelessness remains a national crisis, Bridges to Housing provides a local answer. We remove the financial barrier to permanent housing.

The cost of moving into a home is often the biggest barrier to leaving the streets. While many families can afford monthly rent, they don't have the thousands of dollars needed for a security deposit. Bridges to Housing steps in to cover those upfront costs, deposits and rent, to get Yuba-Sutter-Colusa families into a stable home immediately.

Experience shows that once the barrier of a security deposit is removed, families stay housed. By focusing on this one critical need, we provide the stability families need to thrive. We are proud to help our neighbors in Yuba, Sutter, and Colusa counties secure a foundation and finally have a home of their own.

We provide the "bridge": the deposits and support needed to step into safe housing. That housing is then the door to other forms of support and opportunity.

How we help

A simple process, built around each family's situation.

Each client's story and needs are unique, so we begin by listening. From there, our volunteers and board work with you toward a home suited to your family.

  1. We listen

    We listen without judgment to your story to learn about your current situation, identify your housing needs and challenges, and suggest options. Our Program Administrator helps you assemble the documents for your application and sets a date and time for an interview with a board member or volunteer.

  2. We help you find housing

    Our Housing Navigator guides clients as they locate a suitable residence. We also offer referrals to other community services such as clothing, food, and child support. Once you have found housing, the approval process is expedited.

  3. We cover the deposit

    Once housing has been located, the file is reviewed by the board for approval and appropriate funding. We assist with a rental deposit and/or a first month's rent and landlord incentives, and the funds go directly to the landlord or property owner.
